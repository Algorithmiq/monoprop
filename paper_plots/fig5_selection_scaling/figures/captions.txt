fig5_selection_scaling  --  Generating optimal gates and angles
---------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Generating optimal gates and angles.} Single-core times on an Apple M5 for
candidate generation, occupation-flip table construction, and scoring the generated
candidates, with $(d,g)\in\{(4,2),(4,4),(6,4)\}$ for a random propagated chemistry
Hamiltonian. Legend exponents are fitted over the 4 largest system sizes; dotted guides
show $N^{(d+g)/2}$. Measured over the 4 widest rungs of each case: $d=4$, $g=2$:
$N^{3.07}$ against the $N^{3}$ bound, over $N=96$--192; $d=4$, $g=4$: $N^{4.22}$ against
the $N^{4}$ bound, over $N=64$--160; $d=6$, $g=4$: $N^{5.19}$ against the $N^{5}$ bound,
over $N=32$--80. The time exponents track the fitted pair-count exponents (3.02),
(4.06), (5.19) to within 0.16, and the cost of enumerating one pair stays flat at
9--16~ns across the whole sweep, so selection costs what enumerating the pairs costs and
carries no hidden work on top of it.
